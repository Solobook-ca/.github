## What SoloBook does

- **Bank statement imports & direct sync:** Import RBC (CSV), Wise (CSV), Revolut (CSV), and PrivatBank (XLSX) statements with duplicate protection and rollback, or connect Wise and PrivatBank directly with a read-only token.
- **Automatic CAD conversion:** Foreign-currency transactions (USD, EUR, UAH, and others) are converted to Canadian dollars at official Bank of Canada exchange rates for each transaction date.
- **Business vs. personal separation:** Categorize transactions as business, personal, or mixed, save sorting rules for future imports, and invite your accountant while keeping personal rows private.
- **Invoicing & reports:** Send branded invoices with payment tracking, review monthly and 12-month trends, use a GST/HST worksheet if registered, and export a year bundle for your accountant.
